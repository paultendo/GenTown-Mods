Paultendo v1.6.96

Fifteen additional 32x32 sprites cover places, town affairs, events and work.
The complete set has 35 sprites and 3 clipped condition overlays. GenTown’s
existing sword icon continues to represent conflict. Concept colours retain
their meaning, while object materials, decoration and wear keep their existing
palette system. The atlas, source artwork, prompts and authoring guide are
separate from R74n’s original assets.

Work symbols follow actual missing supplies, other delays, investigation and
completed results. Completed or failed jobs ignore stale delay fields. Surveys
on the move do not acquire a waiting symbol. Council symbols stay beside real
terms and consent controls. Faith and independence records do not create a
physical shrine or an armed war. Lost marks on a building can use ruins, while
lost natural rock stays distinct.

Floods from the water model have known history even without a registry disaster
process. Their symbols survive in that history. Chronicle artwork is rebuilt
from validated story references, rather than words in headlines or saved images.
Hidden records do not gain visible symbols. Reading does not consume randomness,
create residents, change stocks or advance time.

Rendering adds no runtime files or packages. The indexed data, palettes and
renderer stay inside the installable JavaScript. The cache remains bounded at
128 variants. The mod grows by 43,552 bytes compared with v1.6.95.

Desktop and phone inspection covered dark/light sprites, actual/enlarged sizes,
all 90 gallery images, supply and council drawers, highlights, native choices,
a day advance and reload. The visible map survived, answering cleared the waiting
state, and no browser errors or warnings were observed. A native close button’s
3px overflow after returning from mobile was corrected and checked at 1280px.
Phone inspection uses a 390px viewport, not a physical device.

750 campaign turns across seeds 7, 42 and 123 completed without runtime failures
or proposals lacking usable controls. Seed 42 has three unfinished records at
the endpoint. One shipment remains blocked with its real cargo. No forced
completion was used. These campaigns precede only the close-button margin fix.

Both full runs passed all 696 tests. The final run checks the complete release
source, including the close-button correction. The sprite build reproduces its
four outputs exactly. The QA JSON records source hashes, screenshots, campaigns
and publication readback. Human playtesting remains necessary to assess enjoyment and arbitrary
mod combinations.

The craft detector reported no regex findings but ran in degraded mode because
its optional parsers were unavailable. It did not evaluate computed contrast.
