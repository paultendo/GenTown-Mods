V1.6.94 RELEASE VERIFICATION
5 October 2026

Controls and attention

The main day control becomes Decisions when actual proposals are waiting.
It opens their original questions and delegates Yes, No and Act to the
native live controls. Answering does not advance time. Let the day pass
explicitly uses GenTown's varied lapse rules. Urgent letters cannot be
bypassed. Play and speed remain secondary controls.

The Executive groups town life, world, records and time. Projects brings
active research, making, surveys and native construction into one view.
Missing supplies link to actual ingredients, committed stock and local
discoveries. Opening these views consumes no goods, time or random draws.
A maker waiting for unavailable ingredients no longer blocks the research
that could supply them. Such research still requires its materials and work.

Routine sunset totals, repeated tool updates and ordinary mastered batches
fold into Town updates. Their original records remain. New methods,
apprentices, setbacks and live decisions stay visible. Named diagnostics
record repeated failures once, with counts in Info / Game health, alongside
GenTown's original Info entries.

Map and performance

Fog rebuilds retain every town's view instead of clearing earlier towns.
Daily visibility no longer vanishes on unchanged towns. Claimed territory
caches live outside saves, and legacy copies are removed on import/save.
The native compressed map encoding is unchanged. The native serializer
avoids cloning map tiles that it immediately discards. Unchanged Chronicle
serialization is cached. Path searches skip stale queue entries; route
caches account for moved anchors and changed ground, remain bounded and
only cache an unreachable route for that day.

The audit's Day 751 input contained 19 legacy territory caches with 230,264
serialized UTF-8 bytes of duplicate tile data. That is a field total, not
a promised percentage reduction for every save.

Same-input, 30-turn harness timing (CPU-profiled, seed 42, mixed choices):
                    v1.6.93      final v1.6.94
Median nextDay        344.7 ms        305.2 ms
95th percentile       504.6 ms        393.9 ms
Longest call          628.6 ms        623.4 ms

The final median fell 11.5%, with the 95th percentile down 21.9%. Earlier
candidate runs measured 256/257 ms medians. Host load and small samples
vary. These measure native simulation calls in jsdom, not browser FPS.

A real browser turn at Day 756 to 757 also completed with fourfold CPU
throttling: 2.62 seconds of script time, 0.19 seconds of layout and 0.12
seconds of style work. No errors or warnings, and the map remained visible.
The observation includes UI inspection overhead. Slower hardware can still
pause noticeably. This was simulated CPU slowdown, not a physical-device
or network test.

Checks

npm run check passed.
The final full suite passed all 685 tests, with no failures, skips or
cancellations. Eight added regressions cover the decision flow, native
lapse, save encoding, multiple towns' visibility, shortage explanations,
routine-history recovery, real caught failures and research prerequisites.

Three campaigns completed 750 actual turns with no runtime failures,
console errors, warnings or proposals missing controls:
- Seed 42, Yes: 300 turns, Day 1 to 301, 27 discovery thresholds.
- Seed 123, mixed: 300 turns, Day 1 to 301, 15 discovery thresholds.
- Continued seed 42, mixed: 150 turns, Day 751 to 901, nine living towns.

These campaigns used the v1.6.94 simulation build before final choice-button
styling and Info-menu integration corrections. Final release bytes passed
the full suite and real browser checks. Their exact source and input hashes
are retained separately. Work still waiting is retained in the QA record.
The later campaign has six jobs unchanged for at least 30 days. Missing
methods, scarce supplies and unavailable workers remain real constraints.
No artificial completion was inserted to make a campaign look successful.

Browser checks at 1280x720 and 390x844 used an actual imported Day 751 save,
reloads and real interface actions. The map remained visible through Day
757. Act opened its original prompt. A keyboard Yes answered its original
proposal without advancing. The phone Chronicle and work/decision panels
have no horizontal overflow, with 44px touch controls. The native desktop
document retains a 3px width excess; its decision panel fits.

The README has two new genuine captures alongside its earlier story tour.
Capture provenance and image hashes are recorded. No runtime or development
dependencies were added. The independently installable classic script is
still the delivery artifact. The vendor engine, icons and font assets are
unchanged. The earlier other-mod audit remains dated evidence, with a
follow-up describing the implemented repairs.

Human feedback is still needed for pacing, comprehensibility and enjoyment.
These checks cannot certify every save, device or mod combination.
